PL-OS · Business application · Private project

Designing administration around real operational workflows

PL-OS is an administration platform for language-training operations. The objective is to make complex coordination work easier to understand and manage—not just to create a collection of screens.

What the work involves

  • Clarifying operational terminology, roles, permissions, and workflows before deciding what to build.
  • Working through administration concepts such as cohorts, sessions, scheduling, roster management, and reporting.
  • Coordinating AI-assisted implementation with review, iterative feedback, and technical verification.
  • Using automated checks and hands-on review to challenge assumptions about whether a feature is actually usable.
  • Considering integrations, infrastructure, deployment readiness, and ongoing maintenance as part of delivery planning.

What this demonstrates

Product discovery, requirements analysis, workflow modeling, systems thinking, AI-agent direction, QA, and delivery coordination. It also demonstrates a practical distinction: generating code and delivering a dependable business system are not the same task.

Public/private boundary: The application repository, operational details, real student/teacher information, client communications, and infrastructure configuration remain private. The public calendar is a separate visual concept with fictional data; it is not evidence that the entire private platform is complete or deployed.

Website Check · Live public product

Making website feedback accessible

Website Check is a public Switzer Systems service that combines Google PageSpeed Insights mobile measurements with an AI-assisted look at a site's public pages.

Product decisions visible to users

  • Provide useful initial feedback without requiring an email address.
  • Show mobile performance measurements separately from AI-generated observations.
  • Limit the scope of AI review and focus on a short list of potentially useful improvements.
  • State limitations clearly: a point-in-time check is not a full security, accessibility, or performance audit.

What this demonstrates

Translating technical measurement tools into an approachable workflow, service design, user-facing explanations of uncertainty, and the practical operation of a public web tool.

Another Way · AI creative pipeline · Private project

Coordinating a multi-stage graphic-novel workflow

Another Way explores how AI agents and deterministic tools can work together to turn a narrative concept into illustrated graphic-novel pages while maintaining continuity and source-fidelity checks.

01Master arc and chapter plans
02Scene and comic scripts
03Visual prompt preparation
04Illustration and page rendering
05Page and book assembly
06Audit and human review

What this demonstrates

Decomposing a complex creative goal into separately checkable stages, coordinating models and tools, preserving narrative consistency across outputs, and treating validation as an explicit step rather than assuming generated work is correct.

Scope: This is an experimental creative pipeline, not a claim of autonomous end-to-end production quality. The working repository, source text, and project assets remain private. The public Graphic Novel Lessons project linked below is separate.
