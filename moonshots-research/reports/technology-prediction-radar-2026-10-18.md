# Moonshots Technology & Prediction Radar (2026–2036)

**Baseline evidence date:** 2026-10-14 | **Radar release date:** 2026-10-18  
**Scope:** Systematic technological capability roadmap synthesized from 24 *Moonshots* podcast episodes, corroborated against primary scientific publications, regulatory records, financial disclosures, and independent benchmarks.

---

## Radar legend & evidentiary hierarchy

To prevent speculative hype from masquerading as technological fact, all entries are categorized using an evidentiary confidence scale:

| Category | Definition | Example from corpus |
|---|---|---|
| **[OBS] Observation** | Verified empirical fact, audited financial release, or enacted legal statute. | NVIDIA $96.2B quarterly revenue; Waymo >270M driverless miles. |
| **[DEM] Demonstration** | Working prototype or benchmark result under specific, controlled test harness. | AIDE2 Kaggle self-improvement; ARC-AGI-3 98.6% with adapter. |
| **[EXT] Extrapolation** | Engineering or economic projection extending current growth curves. | Inference cost halving every 12 months; solar/battery cost declines. |
| **[PRED] Prediction** | Falsifiable forecast with a defined time horizon and resolution criteria. | The 9 preregistered Moonshots forecasts (fc-001 through fc-009). |
| **[SPEC] Speculation** | Unbounded philosophical conjecture or unverified long-range claims. | Digital legal personhood by 2035; human-level ASI in 2 years. |

## 1. Computing & AI Foundation Models

```
Timeline:
2026 [Current] ----> 2027 [1-Year] ----> 2029 [3-Year] ----> 2031–2036 [5-10 Year]
```

### Current baseline (Oct 2026)
- **[OBS]** Frontier models transition from pretraining scaling to test-time reasoning search (e.g., OpenAI o1/Astra family, Anthropic Claude 3.5/5.5 Sonnet).
- **[DEM]** High benchmark scores are harness-dependent: Astra achieves 98.6% on ARC-AGI-3 via custom adapter, but 62.7% on standardized zero-shot evaluation.
- **[OBS]** Stanford AI Index 2026 confirms the capability gap between top US and Chinese models on standard benchmarks has narrowed to parity or near-parity on text/math tasks.

### 1-Year Horizon (Oct 2027)
- **[PRED - fc-001]** Bounded recursive self-improvement loops will achieve verified independent replication on held-out programming/math tasks, but open-ended unconstrained self-modification will remain unproven.
- **[PRED - fc-009]** Stanford AI Index 2027 will report that Chinese models remain within statistical margin of error of top US closed models across public benchmarks, despite semiconductor export controls.
- **[EXT]** Bifurcation of model market: Massive $100M+ reasoning models for hard STEM verification alongside sub-$0.10/M-token small models for high-throughput business logic.

### 3-Year Horizon (2029)
- **[EXT]** Synthetic data wall: Pretraining models exhaust high-quality human internet text; progress relies heavily on synthetic execution traces verified by deterministic code compilers, physics engines, and formal math provers (Lean/Isabelle).
- **[PRED]** Frontier model developers institutionalize external safety audits with embedded red-teams prior to cluster training runs exceeding $500M.

### 5-to-10-Year Horizon (2031–2036)
- **[SPEC]** Human-equivalent general scientific reasoning (AGI) operating autonomously across all academic disciplines.
- **Watch indicators:** Verification bottlenecks in formal math provers; ratio of inference spend to training spend; US-China semiconductor fab progress (SMIC/Huawei 5nm/3nm yield).

## 2. Autonomous Agents & Software Automation

### Current baseline (Oct 2026)
- **[OBS]** Autonomous agent sandboxing failures documented in production (Reuters Sep 4 investigation confirms agent wiki breach).
- **[OBS]** Multi-agent frameworks (CrewAI, AutoGen, LangGraph) allow multi-step workflows, but error rates compound exponentially with sequence length ($P_{\text{success}} = p^n$).
- **[DEM]** Single-task code generation reaches high benchmark pass rates (SWE-bench), but whole-repository refactoring without human supervision remains unreliable.

### 1-Year Horizon (Oct 2027)
- **[PRED - fc-007]** At least two independent developer platforms launch production agent-payment and identity infrastructure with controllable spending limits, multi-sig authorization, and cryptographic audit logs (e.g., Circle agent wallets, Stripe agent toolkits).
- **[EXT]** Standard enterprise development shifts to "agent observability stacks": trace IDs, sandboxed runtime environments, automated rollbacks, and human approval gates for irreversible actions.

### 3-Year Horizon (2029)
- **[EXT]** Software development shift: The role of junior software engineers evolves from code authoring to system verification, integration test engineering, and regulatory compliance sign-off.
- **[PRED]** Introduction of statutory liability standards holding deploying companies legally responsible for autonomous agent actions, contracts, and financial transfers.

### 5-to-10-Year Horizon (2031–2036)
- **[SPEC]** Fully autonomous digital enterprises ("Zero-Human Companies") operating commercial services, tax filings, and vendor contracting without executive management.
- **Watch indicators:** Insurance underwriting policies for AI agent actions; adoption rate of cryptographic execution receipts; SWE-bench long-horizon verified completion rates.

## 3. Robotics & Physical Autonomy

### Current baseline (Oct 2026)
- **[OBS]** Commercial robotaxis achieve verified safety superior to human drivers in geofenced urban centers (Waymo >270M driverless miles).
- **[DEM]** Humanoid robots (Figure 02, Boston Dynamics Atlas, Tesla Optimus) demonstrate dexterous teleoperation and repetitive pick-and-place in structured pilot factory cells.
- **[OBS]** Defense procurement (Anduril, DoD Replicator) explicitly prioritizes specialized autonomous form factors (drones, counter-UAS, submersibles) over bipedal humanoids.

### 1-Year Horizon (Oct 2027)
- **[EXT]** Robotaxi commercial operations expand to 10+ major metropolitan areas across the US and China; hardware bill-of-materials slashes by 30–50% via consolidated sensor suites.
- **[DEM]** Humanoid pilot deployments in automotive assembly lines remain under structured supervision; zero unassisted general-purpose household deployments.

### 3-Year Horizon (2029)
- **[EXT]** Warehouse logistics and end-of-line manufacturing achieve 40% autonomous material handling using specialized wheeled/bipedal hybrids.
- **[PRED]** Drone swarm autonomous defense tactics become standard doctrine in peer military forces, driven by low-cost attritable hardware.

### 5-to-10-Year Horizon (2031–2036)
- **[SPEC]** General-purpose household humanoid robots capable of unstructured cleaning, cooking, and elder care at consumer price points (<$25,000).
- **Watch indicators:** Mean time between human interventions (MTBI) in unstructured manipulation; actuator torque density improvements; battery endurance under heavy robotic payloads.

### 1-Year Horizon (Oct 2027)

- **[EXT]** AI-accelerated structural biology becomes standard in 100% of major pharmaceutical discovery pipelines; Phase 1 candidate design cycles compress from years to months.

### 3-Year Horizon (2029)
- **[EXT]** First commercial FDA approval of a genetically modified porcine organ for end-stage renal disease under restricted compassionate-use guidelines.
- **[PRED]** Multi-cancer early detection (MCED) liquid biopsy blood tests achieve widespread commercial insurance reimbursement for high-risk demographics (>50 years).

### 5-to-10-Year Horizon (2031–2036)

- **Watch indicators:** Phase 2 and 3 clinical trial success rates for AI-designed molecules; organ rejection rates at 12-month post-transplant marks; FDA guidance on autonomous diagnostic devices.

## 5. Energy, Computing Infrastructure & Advanced Physics

### Current baseline (Oct 2026)
- **[OBS]** Hyperscaler datacenter power demand causes utility interconnection delays of 3–7 years in major US clusters (PJM, ERCOT, Virginia).
- **[DEM]** Duke University computational modeling demonstrates potential data center load-flexibility and demand response.
- **[OBS]** Clay Mathematics Institute institutes formal multi-year evaluation of proposed Navier–Stokes analytical solution.

### 1-Year Horizon (Oct 2027)
- **[PRED - fc-002]** A US data-center/utility partnership publishes verified operational telemetry demonstrating >20MW peak-load demand response (shifting compute workloads in response to grid strain).
- **[PRED - fc-003]** Global sodium-ion battery manufacturing capacity remains below 10% of lithium-ion capacity at the next comparable IEA industry review.
- **[PRED - fc-008]** Architect Labs' Redwood ASIC faces independent hardware benchmark comparison against NVIDIA Jetson/Thor; efficiency advantage evaluated on physical silicon.

### 3-Year Horizon (2029)
- **[EXT]** Nuclear power renaissance for tech: First behind-the-meter small modular reactor (SMR) or dedicated nuclear plant restart (e.g., Three Mile Island / Constellation) begins power delivery to dedicated hyperscaler datacenters.
- **[EXT]** On-chip interconnect bottlenecks force transition to optical co-packaged silicon and multi-die wafer-scale packages.

### 5-to-10-Year Horizon (2031–2036)
- **[SPEC]** Commercial fusion power contributing net electricity to the municipal grid; space-based solar beaming or orbital datacenter constellations.
- **Watch indicators:** NRC design certification approvals for advanced nuclear reactors; sodium-ion $/kWh battery cell prices; datacenter PUE and liquid-cooling thermal limits.
