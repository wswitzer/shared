Jari ya Hamouda
Jari ya Hamouda...Hamouda ya jari daber aâliya yamma...  ya jari daber aâliya yamma ness tebet ergouda....ergouda wani noumi makharoum aâliya yamma
wani noumi makharoum aâliya yamma....  Hamouda ya jari.. ya jari Enta ti li mashaa-li nari yamma Tmanitek fi dari.. fi dari Mesthli  chamma ettol aaleya yamma..  Mesthli  chamma ettol aaleya yammaa  Awel marra ritou.. ah ritou Dekhel ghralbi khabitou yamma..  Dekhel ghralbi khabitou yamma Hani Hanitou.. Hanitou Sametou la-aziz aal ghralbi yamma..  Sametou la-aziz aala ghralbi yamma

English Translation –
My neighbor Hamouda
Oh my neighbor, tell me what to do
While the others sleep,
I cannot sleep
It's you who feeds my fire/passion
Oh Hamouda my neighbor
I dream of you coming to my house
You are like a candle to bring light 
Since the first time I saw him
In my heart I hid him
He comforted me
I named him the dear/love of my heart
