there are (very!) different translations into English, but generally it is:
kazhi mi kak se sadi pipero = tell me how peppers are planted
Da tsafti da varzhe da beresh ka sakash = so they blossom and give fruit that you can pick when you want
Pomunigo, pobutsnigo = you poke it in the soil and push it in
Teta kak se sadi pipero = that's how peppers are planted
It's﻿ been described as a children's song; it's also been interpreted in a, um, more adult sense.
